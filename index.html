CASE STUDY 01 · HIGHRADIUS G4

Creation of a Real-Time Self-Serve Analytics Dashboard

Empowering enterprise finance teams in the Office of the CFO to build custom layouts, visualize live metrics, and act on insights without IT bottlenecks.

ROLE Product Designer (End-to-End)
COMPANY HighRadius (Autonomous Finance)
AUDIENCE Office of the CFO · Finance Teams
SCOPE Research, Flows, UI, Handoff
Overview Problem Process Interactive Demo Impact

01 / OVERVIEW

Context & Domain

HighRadius provides an AI-powered Autonomous Finance Platform for mid-sized and large enterprises across accounts receivable, treasury management, and record-to-report functions. Within the G4 platform, Analytics turns raw operational finance data into actionable insights so leaders can monitor performance, predict trends, and collaborate in real time.

As the Product Designer, I owned the full design lifecycle—from whiteboarding, competitive research, and problem framing to low-fidelity FigJam flows, high-fidelity Figma production using HighRadius’s design system, and JIRA-tracked developer handoff.

02 / THE PROBLEM

Why the "As-Is" Reporting Broke Down

Finance teams were heavily reliant on manual spreadsheets, spending weeks consolidating data across disparate systems. Existing out-of-the-box dashboards functioned as static reporting tools rather than a dynamic self-service platform.

01

Guided but Rigid

Pre-determined dashboards offered quick surface access but lacked flexibility for users to explore data freely or answer custom business questions.

02

Dependent on IT & Vendors

Business users could not build or modify their own charts and layouts, creating engineering bottlenecks and slowing down executive decisions.

03

Siloed & Delayed Data

Lack of real-time synchronization forced teams back into Excel, introducing manual errors, data lag, and missed operational opportunities.

03 / DESIGN PROCESS

From Problem Framing to Production

STEP 01

Understanding & Research

Framed organizational and product constraints. Conducted secondary research on competitor BI tools, audited internal data architecture, mapped the "as-is" flow, and gathered direct user feedback highlighting demand for live, at-a-glance metrics.

STEP 02

Strategy & Wireframing

Formulated the core hypothesis: customizable, real-time dashboards drive faster issue resolution. Mapped end-to-end journeys (login → dashboard → report drill-down) and iterated on low-fidelity layouts collaboratively with PMs and engineers on FigJam.

STEP 03

UI Design & Iteration

Translated validated wireframes into pixel-perfect Figma mockups using HighRadius’s design system. Ran stakeholder feasibility checks and user feedback sessions that directly refined the filtering architecture and primary KPI hierarchy.

STEP 04

Review, Handoff & JIRA

Delivered comprehensive Figma specs, component states, and interactive prototypes. Tracked implementation tasks, edge-case bugs, and design debts in JIRA to ensure clean engineering parity.

HOW I WORK

A pragmatic, 7-step workflow built for clarity and speed.

I always start with pen and paper before touching Figma—resolving information architecture and edge cases early to eliminate unnecessary back-and-forth during high-fidelity production.

01

Receive Requirements

Unpack project briefs, user stories, and constraints with PMs and stakeholders—actively clarifying ambiguities to make sure we solve the right problem.

02

Document Review & End-to-End Flow Analysis

Dive into specs, technical docs, and the live application to trace the user’s full journey—respecting existing context before introducing new features.

03

Paper Sketches & Low-Fidelity Wireframes

Always choose pen and paper first for rough ideation. Prioritizing logic, hierarchy, and ergonomics without getting distracted by pixel-level styling too early.

04

Low-Fidelity Alignment Loop

Present sketches and flows to PMs, engineers, and users to validate the conceptual structure—where changes are fast and inexpensive.

05

High-Fidelity Execution in Figma

Translate approved concepts into crisp, design-system-compliant mockups and interactive prototypes with documented component states and micro-interactions.

06

Internal Engineering & Peer Review

Run a final feasibility and edge-case check with developers and design peers before formal handoff to eliminate surprises during sprint execution.

07

Developer Handoff & QA

Deliver clean specs, interactive prototypes, and JIRA-tracked design tasks so engineering can focus entirely on implementation quality.
